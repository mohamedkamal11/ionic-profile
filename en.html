01

About us

Ionic is an Egyptian factory making architectural and decorative facade elements: cornices, columns and capitals, surrounds, mashrabiya screens, domes, cladding and precast concrete fences. We work from the project drawings and specification, make the master and mould in-house, and supply and install for projects across Egypt according to the contract scope.

The factory started in 2020 making facade elements in glass-fibre reinforced concrete (GRC), then added other materials to suit each element and where it goes: GRP for light pieces and sculptures, GRG for interior decoration, EPS for light mouldings, and precast concrete for fences and structural elements.

01

Why Ionic

What architects, contractors and owners get by working with the factory directly.

01

From drawing to installation under one roof

3D design, shop drawings, moulds, manufacturing, supply, installation and finishing. One party responsible for the element from start to finish, within the contract scope.

02

Moulds made in-house

Every design gets its own master and mould, made by us, so the element matches the drawing and the mould repeats across many pieces with the same accuracy.

03

The right material for each element

GRC, GRP, GRG, EPS and precast concrete. We recommend the material by where the element goes, its weight and its shape, not just by what is available.

04

4 GRC grades with a written warranty

Basic, Standard, Facade and Signature: each grade has its own quality level and warranty period, so offers can be compared grade for grade.

05

Laboratory tests on our own production

Samples of our production were tested at the Housing & Building National Research Center labs: compression, flexure, absorption, alkali resistance and galvanizing thickness of fixing anchors.

06

A strong track record

Mosques and minarets, palaces and villas, residential buildings and compounds, administrative, government and commercial buildings, and a seafront promenade, across several Egyptian governorates. Every project is documented with real photos in our portfolio.

07

To your drawings and the consultant's specification

In all our materials (GRC, GRP, GRG, EPS and precast concrete) we manufacture to the project drawings and the client's or consultant's approved specification: mix proportions, materials, thicknesses, finish and fixings, with samples for approval before production.

08

Installed by our own crew

Our factory crew fixes the elements on site with approved anchors, then treats the joints and finishes them through to handover, within the contract scope.

Vision

Architectural elements made in Egypt with the quality and precision that let architects draw what they want, and find someone to make it.

Mission

We make architectural elements that match the drawings, with traceable materials and tests on our actual production, and we keep to supply and installation dates.

03

Production materials

We choose the material by where the element goes, its weight, shape and required finish.

GRCGlass-fibre Reinforced Concrete

Cement and silica sand reinforced with alkali-resistant (AR) glass fibre, sprayed or cast into moulds to make thin, light pieces with fine detail for exterior facades.

When we use it: Our main choice for most exterior facade elements: durable, weather-resistant, with fine stone-like detail.

GRPGlass-fibre Reinforced Plastic

Glass-fibre reinforced polyester resin — very light and moisture resistant; suited to small domes, sculptures, planters and complex pieces.

When we use it: When very light weight, complex shapes or glossy colours are needed, such as small domes and sculptures.

GRGGlass-fibre Reinforced Gypsum

High-grade gypsum reinforced with glass fibre for interiors: ceilings, interior cornices, inner domes and ornaments.

When we use it: Interiors only: ceilings, interior cornices and inner domes.

GRMPGlass-fibre Reinforced Modified Plaster

Plaster treated with additives that improve its hardness and lower its absorption, reinforced with glass fibre. It gives light pieces with fine detail and a smooth surface, stronger than ordinary plaster.

When we use it: For interior cornices, ornaments, ceilings and inner domes that need more strength and stability than ordinary plaster. Exterior use is decided after testing the mix.

EPSCoated Expanded Polystyrene

EPS foam profiles hot-wire cut, then coated with adhesive base coat, fibre mesh and finish; economical and light for large runs of cornices and surrounds.

When we use it: An economical option for long runs of cornices and surrounds on upper floors.

PrecastPrecast Concrete

Reinforced concrete elements cast in factory moulds, then transported and installed: fence panels and posts, blocks, facade units and landscape elements.

When we use it: For heavy and structural pieces such as fence panels and posts.

04 · What we make

Columns & Capitals

GRC · GRP · GRG

Round and square columns with capitals and bases, usually made in GRC as a shell around the concrete column. The capital follows the project drawing, whether classical, Islamic or Pharaonic.

Uses

  • Cladding existing concrete columns
  • Arcades and entrances of mosques and palaces
  • Villa and building entrances
  • Stand-alone decorative capitals

Finishes

  • Natural white (white cement)
  • Acrylic paint in project colour
  • Stone-like finish

Made to order

Diameter, height and capital pattern per drawings. A master model and mould are made, then the same mould is repeated across many columns.

Handling and storage

Pieces travel upright on pallets with edge protection for carved details, and are stored shaded and away from impact until installation.

For a quotation we need

  • Number of columns
  • Outer diameter and overall height
  • Photo or drawing of capital and base
  • Interior or exterior
  • Supply only or supply & install
